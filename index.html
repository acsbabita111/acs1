Applied Computer School™ is the offline and blended vocational skill-development institution within the FFGPMT education and workforce ecosystem—focused on practical learning, guided practice and employment readiness.

How Training WorksLearn Free Online
Institutional Role

The practical-learning arm of the ACS ecosystem

ACS Learn provides free online learning. Applied Computer School™ focuses on the practical side: trainer-guided learning, hands-on practice, labs, projects, field activities and blended vocational training.

01

Hands-on Practice

Practical tasks, tools, equipment, field work and guided performance—not theory alone.

02

Blended Learning

Free online learning through ACSLEARN™ combined with face-to-face practical sessions where required.

03

Work Readiness

Training designed around useful skills, practical competence, projects, assessment and real-world application.

Our Training Model

Online knowledge. Offline practice. Real competence.

The ACS model separates free access to knowledge from the practical training that requires trainers, tools, labs, workshops, farms, vehicles or other real environments.

1

Learn

Study core concepts online through ACSLEARN™ in an accessible, mobile-first format.

2

Practice

Perform guided tasks in a classroom, lab, workshop, field or other practical setting.

3

Demonstrate

Show the skill through practical assignments, projects, simulations or supervised work.

4

Progress

Build a pathway toward employment, self-employment, advanced learning or specialised training.

Vocational Skill Areas

Practical learning across changing fields of work

Course offerings can expand or change over time according to learner needs, industry demand, available practical facilities and approved programme design.

AI

Digital & AI Skills

Practical digital literacy, AI-enabled work, content creation, online tools and future-ready digital capability.

TS

Technical & Trade Skills

Hands-on vocational learning in technical, repair, fabrication and trade-oriented skill areas.

AG

Agriculture & Livelihood Skills

Practical livelihood-oriented learning linked with farming, food, rural enterprise and local economic activity.

FW

Future Workforce Skills

Emerging skills shaped by changing technologies, employers, mobility systems and new forms of work.

A course appearing online does not automatically mean that practical training is available at every physical location. Centre-wise availability should always be confirmed before enrolment.
