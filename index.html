Who We Are

The practical-learning arm of the ACS ecosystem

ACSLEARN™ provides free online learning. Applied Computer School™ focuses on the practical side—trainer guidance, hands-on practice, labs, projects, field activities, workplace preparation and blended vocational training.

01

Hands-on Practice

Practical tasks, tools, equipment, field work and guided performance—not theory alone.

02

Blended Learning

Free online learning through ACSLEARN™ combined with face-to-face practical sessions where required.

03

Work Readiness

Training built around useful skills, practical competence, projects, assessment and real-world application.

Our Training Model

Online knowledge. Offline practice. Real competence.

The ACS model separates free access to knowledge from the practical training that requires trainers, tools, labs, workshops, farms, vehicles or other real environments.

1

Learn

Study core concepts online through ACSLEARN™.

2

Practice

Perform guided tasks in a classroom, lab, workshop, field or other practical setting.

3

Demonstrate

Show the skill through assignments, projects, simulations or supervised work.

4

Progress

Move toward employment, self-employment, advanced learning or specialised training.
